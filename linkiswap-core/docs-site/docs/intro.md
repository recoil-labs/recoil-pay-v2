# Welcome to RecoilPay

**RecoilPay moves your tokens between blockchains — in plain English.**

There's no route picker, no bridge selection, no chain of confusing screens. You type what you want. RecoilPay works out the rest.

```
swap 1 USDC on OP Sepolia for USDC on Polygon Amoy
```

That's it. Behind that sentence, RecoilPay:

1. Reads what you asked for.
2. Finds the best price across independent solvers.
3. Waits for you to confirm and sign.
4. Delivers the tokens on the destination chain.

You never see a bridge. You never see a DEX. You just get your funds.

## What RecoilPay is not

- **Not a custodian.** RecoilPay never holds your funds. Every move is signed from your wallet, on your device.
- **Not a bank.** You are always in control. You can walk away between any two steps.
- **Not mainnet yet.** RecoilPay is running on public **testnets** — test networks where the tokens have no real-world value. It's live, it's real software, but you're moving play money for now.

If any of that surprises you, jump straight to the [FAQ](./faq).
